## 📊 Market Overview & Industry Structure 📈

The global note-taking and knowledge management software market is estimated at **$7.5 Billion to $8.2 Billion** and is projected to surpass **$16 Billion by 2032** growing at a CAGR of ~11.5%. The sector exhibits **moderate fragmentation**, featuring tech hyperscalers (*Microsoft*, *Apple*) providing bundled productivity suits alongside specialized high-growth platforms (*Notion*, *Obsidian*, *Bending Spoons/Evernote*), while a thriving ecosystem of privacy-first, local-first open-source projects caters to developers and security-conscious users.
